# Data, products, and fine detail

These poster notes are part of the [36-example library review](../example-library-review.md).

## Data

| Category | Example | Disposition | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data | [City Network Pulses](../../src/agent_studio/examples/city-network-pulses/scene.py) | Keep | The warm–cool route split works. Bright riders bunch at the middle transfer and mask a little local route detail at gallery scale. |
| Data | [Market Town Agents](../../src/agent_studio/examples/market-town-agents/scene.py) | Keep | The lifted plaza ties the miniature's arrangements together, with different stalls and enough surrounding rooftops to show circulation. Figures are small in the poster and clearer in the later descent. |
| Data | [Population Bars Time](../../src/agent_studio/examples/population-bars-time/scene.py) | Keep | Indexed value cards and the high year counter keep five series readable when the perspective compresses the axes. The blue gallery and off-center relief separate it from the dark transit routes. |

## Micro/macro

| Category | Example | Disposition | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro/macro | [Cell Membrane](../../src/agent_studio/examples/cell-membrane/scene.py) | Keep | The low oblique view exposes both lipid layers, with sparse warm proteins standing out from dense blue material. |
| Micro/macro | [DNA Helix Orbit](../../src/agent_studio/examples/dna-helix-orbit/scene.py) | Keep | The tall alternating rungs and colored backbones differentiate it from other cool microscopic subjects and use the vertical frame. |
| Micro/macro | [Snowflake Growth](../../src/agent_studio/examples/snowflake-growth/scene.py) | Keep | A centered cyan crystal against coarse blue background remains distinct from the filament-like jellyfish. The first arms read at small size. |

## Product

| Category | Example | Disposition | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product | [Headphones Exploded View](../../src/agent_studio/examples/headphones-exploded-view/scene.py) | Keep | Major assemblies read cleanly on white. Some of the palest thin connections disappear at gallery size. |

## Typography

| Category | Example | Disposition | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typography | [Kinetic Type Manifesto](../../src/agent_studio/examples/kinetic-type-manifesto/scene.py) | Keep | Cream relief lettering and orange, yellow and turquoise plates stay distinct on the blue cabinet. The cover catches THINGS entering from the left and the finished three-word stack reads in the later hold. |
| Typography | [Neon Sign Sting](../../src/agent_studio/examples/neon-sign-sting/scene.py) | Improve | The default poster originally showed pink OPEN while the cyan LATE tubes were barely lit. The full set remains visually distinct from Noir. |
| Typography | [Noir Title Card](../../src/agent_studio/examples/noir-title-card/scene.py) | Improve | The default poster originally showed convincing hard blind stripes with barely a hint of the title. Retain the slow push and early graphic atmosphere while bringing the title up sooner. |
