# Motion and characters

These poster notes are part of the [36-example library review](../example-library-review.md).

## Abstract

| Category | Example | Disposition | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abstract | [Flow-Field Ribbons](../../src/agent_studio/examples/flow-field-ribbons/scene.py) | Keep | Offset cyan and pink ribbons curl through the frame at different scales. The cropped strands and open central channel imply a camera in the flow. |
| Abstract | [Fractured Sphere Breath](../../src/agent_studio/examples/fractured-sphere-breath/scene.py) | Keep | Pale ceramic edges stay distinct from the orange fissures. Soft gray surround keeps the inner glow from reading like another space scene. |
| Abstract | [Pendulum Wave](../../src/agent_studio/examples/pendulum-wave/scene.py) | Keep | The steep diagonal places all the bobs on a readable axis. The thinnest wires need the full-size poster. |
