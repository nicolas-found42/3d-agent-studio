# Places and environments

These poster notes are part of the [36-example library review](../example-library-review.md).

## Architecture

| Category | Example | Disposition | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Architecture | [Brutalist Courtyard Noon](../../src/agent_studio/examples/brutalist-courtyard-noon/scene.py) | Keep | Portrait crop carries the high facade. Hard shadows, green foliage and pool reflections give scale without muddying the structure. |
| Architecture | [Gothic Nave Dawn](../../src/agent_studio/examples/gothic-nave-dawn/scene.py) | Keep | Amber shafts and blue–red glass anchor the long symmetrical aisle. Shadowed ceiling ribs remain subtle at gallery size. |
| Architecture | [Paper City Isometric](../../src/agent_studio/examples/paper-city-isometric/scene.py) | Keep | A diagonal isolated slab, thin pastel skins and small streets make the paper model distinct from the two full-frame interiors. |

## Landscape

| Category | Example | Disposition | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landscape | [Alpine Lake Sunrise](../../src/agent_studio/examples/alpine-lake-sunrise/scene.py) | Keep | Unbroken twilight reflection ties the low viewpoint to the texture of the peaks. The ridge silhouette and dim forest separate in three depth planes. |
| Landscape | [Bamboo Grove Rain](../../src/agent_studio/examples/bamboo-grove-rain/scene.py) | Keep | Soft lantern glow and sharp near-green stalk make the focus shift intelligible. The lantern comes into focus later in the clip. |
| Landscape | [Desert Dunes Moonlight](../../src/agent_studio/examples/desert-dunes-moonlight/scene.py) | Keep | Pink and amber warmth stays local to the campfire. Rippled sand and cold hills give the narrow night frame layers. |

## Space

| Category | Example | Disposition | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Space | [Black Hole Accretion](../../src/agent_studio/examples/black-hole-accretion/scene.py) | Keep | Hot orange rings and a clean central void contrast with the other planetary landscapes. |
| Space | [Lunar Landing](../../src/agent_studio/examples/lunar-landing/scene.py) | Keep | The fitted gold lower module and narrow landing far legs read against black. The small Earth and hard ground shadows establish the low surface point of view. |
| Space | [Ringed Planet Flyby](../../src/agent_studio/examples/ringed-planet-flyby/scene.py) | Keep | Strong bands on the cropped giant and close ring rocks support the flyby perspective. Crisp, austere light makes the edges readable. |

## Still life

| Category | Example | Disposition | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Still life | [Breakfast Flatlay](../../src/agent_studio/examples/breakfast-flatlay/scene.py) | Keep | True top view, pastel linens and distinct plateware stand apart from the warm directional still lifes. |
| Still life | [Ceramics Studio Morning](../../src/agent_studio/examples/ceramics-studio-morning/scene.py) | Keep | Rows of handmade glazes build horizontal rhythm. Pale window light settles around the lower workbench without hiding the pieces. |
| Still life | [Dutch Still Life](../../src/agent_studio/examples/dutch-still-life/scene.py) | Keep | Narrow chiaroscuro framing puts metallic and glass highlights among fruit and fabric. The foreground table stays readable under the deep falloff. |
